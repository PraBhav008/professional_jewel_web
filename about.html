About

A workshop with a story

Founded on the belief that jewelry should be personal, lasting and honestly made.

Small studio, big attention to detail

Aurelle started as a two-person bench and grew by word of mouth. We still design, cast, set and polish every piece ourselves.

We choose fewer designs and make them better, so what you wear today still looks right in thirty years.
